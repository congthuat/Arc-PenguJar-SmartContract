# PenguJar Arc Testnet Demo Checklist

Use dedicated testnet wallets and testnet USDC only. Never expose seed phrases or private keys in the recording.

## Before recording

- [ ] Production URL is available over HTTPS.
- [ ] Contract address and ArcScan links are correct.
- [ ] OKX Wallet is installed and unlocked on Arc Testnet.
- [ ] Demo wallets have sufficient testnet USDC and native gas.
- [ ] Browser notifications, personal bookmarks, and unrelated extensions are hidden.
- [ ] English/Vietnamese and Light/Dark controls work.

## Suggested flow

1. Open PenguJar and explain the goal-based USDC savings model.
2. Connect OKX Wallet and confirm the connected network is Arc Testnet.
3. Create one jar with a target and future unlock time.
4. Deposit a small testnet USDC amount as the owner.
5. Share or copy the canonical public jar URL.
6. Open that URL in a private/disconnected browser and show public state and Activity.
7. Connect a second wallet and review the contributor-rights warning.
8. Contribute a small testnet USDC amount.
9. Show the confirmed contribution in progress, accounting, Activity, and ArcScan.
10. Explain that target completion never bypasses the time lock.
11. Show closed Jar #3 as a historical example with zero balance and withdrawal Activity.
12. Open the verified PenguJarV2 contract and one real transaction on ArcScan.

## Safety notes

- Contributors cannot withdraw.
- Only the owner can withdraw after unlock.
- Do not attempt an early withdrawal merely for presentation.
- Never approve unlimited USDC; the interface requests the exact amount.
- Stop if the wallet, chain ID, contract address, or amounts differ from the review screen.
